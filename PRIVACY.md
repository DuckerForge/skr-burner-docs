# Privacy Policy - SKR Burner

This Privacy Policy explains how SKR Burner handles data. We prioritize your anonymity and the decentralized nature of the Solana blockchain.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect personal information (names, emails, or phone numbers). However, to provide the Leaderboard and Giveaway features, we process:
* **Public Wallet Addresses:** Your Solana public key is used to track burn amounts and NFT ownership.
* **Transaction Metadata:** Amount of $SKR burned and SOL paid for minting.
* **Firebase Database:** We store partially masked wallet addresses and burn statistics in a Firebase real-time database to power the global leaderboard.

## 2. Data Transparency
* **On-Chain Data:** All transactions are public on the Solana blockchain and can be viewed via explorers like Solscan.
* **Leaderboard:** Your wallet is masked (e.g., `Abc1***xyz9`) to provide a layer of privacy while maintaining competitive transparency.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use the following services to run the dApp:
* **Helius RPC:** To interact with the Solana network.
* **Firebase:** To store leaderboard data and total burn statistics.
* **CoinGecko API:** To provide real-time price data for $SKR and SOL.
* **Pinata (IPFS):** To host NFT metadata and images.

## 4. Cookies and Tracking
The SKR Burner dApp does not use cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics (like Google Analytics).

## 5. Contact
For support or community inquiries, please reach out via our official X (Twitter) profile.
